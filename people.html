Who we are

Dr. Chaiyong Ragkhitwetsagul

Lab director · Lecturer in Software Engineering

He received his Ph.D. in Software Engineering from University College London, where he was part of the CREST centre. He holds a master's degree from Carnegie Mellon University and a bachelor's degree in Computer Engineering from Kasetsart University.

His research covers a wide range of topics in software engineering, including code similarity and clone detection, coding proficiency, and the robustness of language models for code. He has published in TSE, EMSE, JSS, ASE, MSR, and ICSME.

He was previously at the Faculty of ICT, Mahidol University, where he co-founded the SERU research group and is now building CURSOR at the Department of Computer Engineering, Faculty of Engineering, Chulalongkorn University.

Chaiyong also hosts SE Corner, a weekly software engineering podcast in Thai.
